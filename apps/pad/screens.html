RaceBall pad screens

Every screen of the phone controller, drawn with the real src/style.css. The markup under each screen is the contract for the pad code. Set the player's color with style="--player: …" on the screen, and their shape with data-shape. On the racing screen, press the zones and use the buttons to try the event cues.
